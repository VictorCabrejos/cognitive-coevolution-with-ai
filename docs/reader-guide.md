# Chapter practice resources

Simple English companion practice for Cognitive Co-Evolution with AI by Victor M. Cabrejos Jr., PhD.

[Browse all twelve chapters](../chapters/README.md) · [Book source map and coverage](../source-map.md)

Each chapter provides a short guide, a cheat sheet, one 15–20 minute practice activity, five self-check questions and explained answers with an activity rubric. Read the relevant book chapter, keep a first attempt, inspect the evidence, try a changed case and review the reasoning.

No programming, paid account or upload is required. Use the fictional information supplied. Do not substitute personal, student or confidential workplace records. Where an assistant is available, it can challenge an attempt; it does not replace your own source check or independent transfer task. Without one, these activities rehearse parts of the method rather than establish that a full human–SI Vortex occurred.

The book uses SI for Super Intelligence in its discussion of assistants; the terminology alone makes no new claim about a particular tool's capabilities. The Vortex can develop through long, substantive domain-learning conversations across encounters. A short activity is a starting point, not a claim that deeper inquiry is merely a change in immediate priorities or a count of chat messages.

Use conceptual terms as the author's framework. Keep observations separate from hypotheses, aspirations and empirical findings. The questions examine understanding and application, not personal agreement with the philosophy. A self-review score is a practice aid, not a validated measure of learning or competence.
