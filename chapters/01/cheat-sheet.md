## Key distinctions

**Operative Augmentation.** Assistance improves execution while the person supervises, evaluates and steers.

**Cognitive Augmentation.** The person's understanding or judgment develops through the work; a stronger artifact alone does not show this.

**Cognitive Friction.** Avoidable difficulty can be reduced while preserving useful effort such as recall, comparison and application.

**Neuroplastic Transformation.** The framework's aspiration for durable changes in thinking. A single successful explanation does not demonstrate biological change or lasting retention.

## Everyday example

A housemate uses an assistant to organize a shopping list, checks it against the meal plan and corrects a missing ingredient. That can be Operative Augmentation. Independently adapting the list for a changed number of guests supplies separate evidence of understanding.
