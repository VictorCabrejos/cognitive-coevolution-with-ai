## Explained answers

### 1. C

The fallback is useful evidence about control in that test. It cannot establish evaluation independence or source quality.

### 2. A

Attribution and evidence remain different duties. The reference needs repair; intent is not established by the gap alone.

### 3. B

Accessibility can change the demonstration format while preserving the competence being assessed.

### 4. Sample answer

State that the 20 cases were also used during development, so they do not establish independent evaluation. Preserve how they were selected and used, the results they actually describe, the limitation and a proposed separate check. Keep the fallback demonstration as a distinct supported finding.

### 5. Sample answer

Preserve the actual response, the published criterion, competing interpretations, the unresolved question and the next evidence or review action. Disagreement alone establishes neither deception nor universal failure. The chapter's institutional account remains a bounded hypothesis rather than a prevalence result.

## Activity self-review

### One defensible response

Evidence: the independence claim is unsupported because development cases were reused. Control: the fallback was demonstrated under one changed condition. Attribution: assistance was disclosed. Rationale: the added value of three assistants remains untested without a simpler comparison. A defensible review corrects the claim, retains these separate findings and offers a manageable changed-case explanation task before a broader decision.

### Five-point review

Give yourself one point for each criterion your work demonstrates. A different conclusion can earn the point when it meets the criterion and is supported by the supplied information.

1. All six assessment dimensions receive a relevant observation or explicit unknown.
2. The reused-case limitation is recorded accurately.
3. The fallback and disclosure are retained as separate findings.
4. The ownership check names a capability and equivalent accessible formats.
5. The decision gives an achievable repair without inventing intent or universal conclusions.

**4–5:** identify one limit or improvement before reusing the work. **2–3:** revise one missing element and explain the change. **0–1:** return to the source card and work through one condition before expanding the task.

This is a practice aid, not a validated measure of competence, learning or health. Evaluate the reasoning rather than agreement with a philosophy or confidence in a response.
