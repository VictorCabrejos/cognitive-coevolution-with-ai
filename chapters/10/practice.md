## Review a project without grading its polish

**Time:** about 18 minutes. **Materials:** paper or a plain document; an assistant is optional.

### Source card

Fictional submission: a team uses three assistants to sort invented service requests. Its report calls 20 cases an independent evaluation, but the same 20 were used during development. The owner demonstrates a fallback when one tool is unavailable. Assistance is disclosed. No comparison with a simpler design is supplied.

### Steps

1. Criteria (4 minutes): make six short rows: purpose, evidence, rationale, control, uncertainty and attribution. Note what the submission supports or leaves open in each.
2. Trace (4 minutes): identify the evaluation claim that must change. Preserve the demonstrated fallback as a different finding rather than treating one as proof of the other.
3. Ownership check (4 minutes): propose one changed-case task that the owner can explain in writing or speech. State the capability it tests and what evidence would meet the same criterion in either format.
4. Repair (3 minutes): request an achievable correction to the evaluation wording and a bounded plan for a separate check or a simpler comparison. Do not require an unsupported accusation or extra complexity.
5. Decision record (3 minutes): write what can be accepted now, what remains unresolved and what evidence could change the decision. Separate a report error from an inference about intent.

### Expected deliverable

A six-dimension review, one fair ownership task and a short decision with a bounded repair request.

### Reflection checklist

- [ ] Have I kept fallback control separate from evaluation independence?
- [ ] Does disclosure remain different from claim verification?
- [ ] Can the proposed task be attempted in more than one accessible format?
- [ ] Does the review describe the evidence gap without inventing misconduct?

Keep your first attempt before consulting the [sample response and rubric](answers.md#activity-self-review). You can pause, save an open question and return later.
