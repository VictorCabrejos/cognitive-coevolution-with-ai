## Key distinctions

**Judgment Bootstrap (Problem).** A beginner needs help judging the very assistance intended to develop judgment. A small inspectable claim gives a starting point.

**Grounding / Source Anchoring.** Make the explanation answerable to a relevant passage or observation. Agreement between assistants is not an independent source check.

**Cognitive Translation.** Make dense material usable while preserving important conditions and uncertainty.

**Minimum Viable Vortex.** One human, one accessible SI tool, one real problem, and a loop of clarification, evaluation, application and preserved learning. Paper practice can rehearse parts of this method.

## Everyday example

An instruction says new members need a form but renewals are exempt. “Everyone needs a form” is shorter, but changes the rule. A clearer explanation must preserve the distinction.

## Keep this question in view

Does every claimed requirement appear in the source?
