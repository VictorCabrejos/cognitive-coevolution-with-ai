## Check a rule and its exceptions

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Materials:** paper or a plain document; an assistant is optional.

### Source card

Fictional membership card: “New members submit the registration form. Renewing members do not submit it unless their contact details have changed.” A proposed summary says, “Every member must submit a form.” Use fictional member labels only.

### Steps

1. Read (3 minutes): identify the people covered by the rule and both conditions affecting renewals.
2. Correct (3 minutes): write a two-sentence explanation and point to the source words supporting each sentence.
3. Classify (3 minutes): decide what the rule says for a new member, an unchanged renewal and a renewal with changed contact details. Do not invent a submission deadline.
4. Transfer (4 minutes): close your explanation. New rule: “New volunteers attend orientation. Returning volunteers attend only if assigned a new role.” Explain three corresponding cases independently.
5. Preserve (2 minutes): record one checked statement, one possible implication and one unknown. An optional assistant may challenge your translation after your first attempt; return to the rule to decide.

### Expected deliverable

A corrected explanation, three membership decisions, three changed-case decisions and an evidence note.

### Reflection checklist

- [ ] Does every claimed requirement appear in the source?
- [ ] Did I keep the exception attached to the rule?
- [ ] Have I distinguished a possible implication from a checked statement?
- [ ] Can I apply the structure with the first explanation closed?

Keep your first attempt before consulting the [sample response and rubric](answers.md#activity-self-review). You can pause, save an open question and return later.
