## Key distinctions

**Objective Function (the human as).** The person determines what matters, what quality means and when to stop, including relevant human limits.

**Engineered Centrality.** Evidence, review time and effective power to revise, reject or stop make human judgment consequential.

**Human as Semantic Center.** Preserve the meaning of a requirement and the people it serves, rather than treating it as an empty label.

**Human Checkpoint.** Review a concrete result before consequential acceptance or action. A recommendation and permission to commit are different.

## Everyday example

A library event needs step-free access. A venue with an attractive price remains unconfirmed until access is checked. Access is a condition of eligibility; price can guide a comparison between eligible venues.
