*Memory that survives the conversation*

## Key distinctions

**Semantic Checkpointing.** Save the meaning and reasons behind the current state. Decision / Basis / Open / Next is a compact practical form, not a limit on needed context.

**Context Rehydration.** Restore relevant current sources, accepted decisions, corrections, limits and the authorized next task; then inspect whether the resulting work follows them.

**Evidence Discipline / Evidence Tiers.** Keep [VERIFIED] directly checked statements within their scope; [CORPUS] reports preserved in source material; [MEMORY] recalled context; and [INFERENCE] interpretations distinguishable. A source report is not independent confirmation of its truth. A summary must not silently turn an impression into a cause. Mark missing support as unknown.

**Cognitive Sovereignty.** Preserve understandable memory, criteria, files and learning outside one platform, so you can revise, resume, choose another helper or leave. An unexplained archive is weaker protection.

## Everyday example

A fictional reading-club note says, “We kept two questions because the meeting lasts 20 minutes.” Saving only “Use two” loses the condition. A later organizer needs the reason to decide whether a longer meeting changes the choice.

## Keep this question in view

Does the record explain why the decision was made?
