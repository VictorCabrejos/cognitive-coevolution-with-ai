## Activity self-review

### One defensible response

Decision: one example with two modifications. Basis: limited practice time and an untaught prerequisite for the extension. Open: whether learners can explain the modifications independently; no trial yet. Next: correct the worksheet's five modifications to match the accepted scope, then prepare a small trial. ### Five-point review

Give yourself one point for each criterion your work demonstrates. A different conclusion can earn the point when it meets the criterion and is supported by the supplied information.

1. All four checkpoint fields are meaningful.
2. The basis includes both original reasons.
3. The learner outcome is not presented as tested.
4. The handoff identifies the worksheet mismatch and a check of actual output.
5. The changed condition is recorded without erasing still-relevant constraints or history.

**4–5:** identify one limit or improvement before reusing the work. **2–3:** revise one missing element and explain the change. **0–1:** return to the source card and work through one condition before expanding the task.

This is a practice aid, not a validated measure of competence, learning or health. Evaluate the reasoning rather than agreement with a philosophy or confidence in a response.
