## Key distinctions

**The foundational Vortex.** A person contributes to an inquiry, examines assistance, challenges reasons, applies and reflects. The purpose can be deep domain understanding, developed across encounters, rather than only changing an immediate preference.

**Vortex of Vortexes.** Purposeful inquiries are intentionally connected around the human's goals. What transfers must remain relevant, supported and bounded by the original contexts.

**Delegated Cognitive Orchestration.** One or more coordinating SIs proactively organize work across systems and purposeful Vortices, surface relevant connections and decisions, and proceed within human-set scope. The human continues substantive inquiry with them and retains purpose, criteria, correction, review and authority.

**Organization, memory and authority.** The conceptual 1D–4D progression describes organization, not literal dimensions or an empirical ranking. It differs from the Triple Evolution Model and additional AI-to-AI Evolution layer. Rich memory can help continuity but cannot itself make refusal effective; Engineered Centrality needs controls that change conduct.

## Everyday example

A fictional reading inquiry asks what a memo measured; a teaching inquiry asks how to help learners check claims. A permitted shared note carries the measurement distinction between them. It does not carry private participants' records or grant authority to publish a recommendation.
