## Connect two inquiries and keep the reasons visible

**Time:** about 20 minutes. **Materials:** paper or a plain document; an assistant is optional.

### Source card

Fictional reading memo: two evening sessions recorded 40 visits in total; two morning sessions recorded 20. An initial recommendation says, “Evening sessions doubled books read; cancel morning sessions.” A separate teaching inquiry is developing an exercise about activity measures versus outcomes. Only the invented example and its reasoning may be shared between these inquiries; no publication or contacting participants is authorized.

### Steps

1. Initial inquiry (4 minutes): identify what was counted, what was inferred and what would be needed to support the recommendation. Contribute your own tentative explanation before asking for feedback.
2. Develop understanding (5 minutes): formulate two connected questions about why an activity count may fail to establish an outcome. Use an available assistant to examine one challenge, or rehearse the reasoning on paper. A short start can become a longer domain-learning conversation over later encounters.
3. Connect (4 minutes): sketch the reading and teaching inquiries as a Vortex of Vortexes. Write the permitted shared note. Then describe what a coordinating SI could do proactively under Delegated Cognitive Orchestration and what decision still returns to the human.
4. Transfer (4 minutes): close the memo correction. New case: a fictional garden log lists three watering sessions last week and six this week but records no water quantity. Explain independently what doubled and what did not become established.
5. Checkpoint (3 minutes): record Decision / Basis / Open / Next. Specify an effective stop or refusal condition for any next action and a substantive question for continued study. Label the network sketch as a design exercise, not evidence that a system ran.

### Expected deliverable

A corrected source claim, a two-inquiry sketch, a bounded shared note, an independent transfer explanation and a checkpoint.

### Reflection checklist

- [ ] Did the activity measure remain different from the outcome?
- [ ] Does the connection preserve separate purposes and information boundaries?
- [ ] Does human correction change both the work and its next permitted action?
- [ ] Have I preserved a question for deeper study without claiming permanent learning or guaranteed future centrality?

Keep your first attempt before consulting the [sample response and rubric](answers.md#activity-self-review). You can pause, save an open question and return later.
