## Key distinctions

**The Vortex.** A purposeful problem develops through assistance, human evaluation, correction, application and preserved learning. The person contributes questions and reasons.

**Depth within the author's proposal.** Sustained absorption or flow is proposed as necessary for deeper CCEwAI development. This is a conceptual position, not an empirical law of all learning. Depth can continue across encounters and includes correction and voluntary stopping.

**Three evolutions and an additional fourth layer.** The original model concerns SI tools developing, the working arrangement adapting to its human, and the human developing. AI-to-AI Evolution adds systems developing contributions through human-directed exchange; contextual adaptation does not establish model retraining.

**Capability Routing and Vortex of Vortexes.** Give a contributor a specific job. Intentionally connect purposeful inquiries across life domains when relevant, retaining their separate evidence, aims and context boundaries.

## Everyday example

A fictional reader studies why a score ranking may not settle a practical decision. They ask what the score measures, examine a counterexample and later apply the distinction to a course survey. A long transcript alone would not demonstrate that learning.
