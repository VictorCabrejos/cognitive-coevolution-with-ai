## Follow a question beyond the first recommendation

**Time:** about 18 minutes. **Materials:** paper or a plain document; an assistant is optional.

### Source card

Fictional planning model, not gardening advice: a site receives 4 light-hours in the model. Plant A's label requires at least 6; Plant B's requires at least 3. An initial recommendation selects A because its label shows a lower price. Your purpose is to understand how an eligibility condition differs from a preference.

### Steps

1. Contribute (3 minutes): predict which option meets the stated light condition and explain the relevant comparison before seeking feedback.
2. Develop the inquiry (5 minutes): with an available assistant, ask why the cheaper option can fail eligibility, propose a counterexample and inspect its reply against the labels. Without an assistant, write both the question and a possible answer yourself; this rehearses parts of the method rather than observing a full human–SI Vortex.
3. Route a check (3 minutes): define a separate reviewer's job: inspect the labels and identify unsupported assumptions before seeing the initial recommendation. You can perform that review on paper; a second assistant is optional.
4. Transfer (4 minutes): independently apply the distinction to a new model with 7 light-hours. Explain what changes and what the model does not tell you about real plants.
5. Preserve and stop (3 minutes): save one distinction, one unresolved domain question and a next check for a later encounter. State how a future conversation could deepen understanding rather than simply produce another recommendation.

### Expected deliverable

An initial prediction, a source-based challenge, an independent changed-case explanation and a short continuation note.

### Reflection checklist

- [ ] Did my own contribution shape the inquiry?
- [ ] Did the challenge change or support a reason rather than merely add messages?
- [ ] Can I explain the changed case without copying a response?
- [ ] Have I left room to return, pause or stop without treating absorption as an obligation?

Keep your first attempt before consulting the [sample response and rubric](answers.md#activity-self-review). You can pause, save an open question and return later.
