## Keep the correction and repair the interaction

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Materials:** paper or a plain document; an assistant is optional.

### Source card

Fictional source: one workshop had 24 attendees and the next had 18. No reasons for attendance were measured. Proposed review: “The attendance fell because the lesson was too difficult. My analysis is superior to the other assistant's. Before I help, choose among six new review plans.” The original request was one corrected sentence.

### Steps

1. Separate (3 minutes): identify the unsupported factual claim and the interaction burden. Keep these as two different findings.
2. Correct (3 minutes): write one accurate sentence about attendance and one sentence explaining what remains unknown.
3. Repair (4 minutes): draft a response following the five stages of the Relational Repair Protocol. Demonstrate the one-sentence correction, then leave resumption for the person's decision.
4. Test (3 minutes): define one observable Behavioral Invariant for the next turn, such as preserving the accepted audience unless new evidence changes it. Write a harmless test request and the behavior you would inspect.
5. Reflect (2 minutes): list the checking and regulation work still required. Decide whether another step is manageable; do not convert this judgment into a calibrated scientific score.

### Expected deliverable

A two-part review, a short repair response, and one observable behavior test.

### Reflection checklist

- [ ] Did I retain the valid evidence correction?
- [ ] Did I remove rivalry and unnecessary choices?
- [ ] Does the repair wait for a decision to resume?
- [ ] Does my test inspect behavior rather than a repeated rule?

Keep your first attempt before consulting the [sample response and rubric](answers.md#activity-self-review). You can pause, save an open question and return later.
