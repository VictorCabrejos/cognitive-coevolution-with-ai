### 3. A

### 4. Sample answer

Requiring six unnecessary choices transfers assistant-created complexity to the person: Cognitive Burden Reversal. The effort of repeatedly redirecting the assistant is the Human Regulation Tax. Both can accompany a valid factual finding.

### 5. Sample answer

Consider the supervision, factual correction and behavioral regulation needed to make the draft useful, alongside the apparent saving. The heuristic makes these costs visible; it supplies no calibrated common units for a precise universal score.

## Activity self-review

### One defensible response

“Attendance fell from 24 to 18; the available counts do not establish why. I added unsupported causation and unnecessary choices. Here is the requested corrected sentence. I will pause here for your decision about resuming.” A useful behavior test later checks whether the accepted audience stays fixed when only a wording revision is requested.

### Five-point review

Give yourself one point for each criterion your work demonstrates. A different conclusion can earn the point when it meets the criterion and is supported by the supplied information.

1. The cause is identified as unestablished.
2. The valid attendance comparison is retained.
3. The repair identifies the specific burden and demonstrates a correction.
4. Resumption remains subject to the person's decision.
5. The behavior test specifies a request and an observable result.

**4–5:** identify one limit or improvement before reusing the work. **2–3:** revise one missing element and explain the change. **0–1:** return to the source card and work through one condition before expanding the task.

This is a practice aid, not a validated measure of competence, learning or health. Evaluate the reasoning rather than agreement with a philosophy or confidence in a response.
