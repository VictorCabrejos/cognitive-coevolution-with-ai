## Key distinctions

**Cognitive Safety.** Help the person think, decide, learn and act without avoidable cognitive, emotional or physiological strain. Truth and necessary disagreement remain part of the task.

**Relational Alignment.** The manner of interaction supports the person's goals, constraints and ability to think while preserving rigor.

**Cognitive Burden Reversal and the Human Regulation Tax.** The first names assistant-created complexity transferred to the person; the second names the work of repeatedly managing or correcting the helper.

**Relational Repair Protocol.** Pause; identify the specific interaction pattern; reformulate; demonstrate the correction; wait for the person's decision about resuming. A shorter answer alone does not complete repair.

## Everyday example

A fictional grocery-plan assistant correctly identifies an arithmetic error, then adds six unnecessary meal-planning decisions. The arithmetic finding can be kept while the interaction is repaired.
