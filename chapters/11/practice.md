# Chapter 11 practice

*Build something that gives time back*

## Make a reusable card earn its place

**Time:** about 18 minutes. **Materials:** paper or a plain document; an assistant is optional.

### Source card

Fictional timing card: preparation previously took 90 minutes. In one assisted use, drafting takes 20 minutes and checking/adaptation/repair takes 50. In a second hypothetical use, drafting takes 15, checking/adaptation takes 35 and support takes 10. These are examples for arithmetic, not predictions of your own savings.

### Steps

1. Count (4 minutes): calculate accepted-work time and the saving for both uses. Keep each workload category visible.
2. Build (5 minutes): create a short reusable source-checking card for a fictional claim: “A poll of confidence proves a skill gain.” Include what the reader should inspect and what the source would need to establish the claim.
3. Use note (4 minutes): add audience, purpose, order of use, source needs, limits and review triggers. State whether a first attempt should happen before a suggested correction is revealed.
4. Portability test (3 minutes): read the card without imagining access to its original chat. Identify one ambiguity a colleague would face and repair it. An optional assistant may question its instructions after your own review.
5. Choose (2 minutes): record whether the card is worth another small trial, needs simplification or should be set aside. Consider effort and human capacity as well as the number of outputs.

### Expected deliverable

Two time calculations, a reusable source-checking card, a use note and a bounded next decision.

### Reflection checklist

- [ ] Did I include checking, adaptation and support?
- [ ] Does the asset explain its source needs and order of use?
- [ ] Can another reader use it without the old conversation?
- [ ] Have I kept a small trial distinct from guaranteed savings or business demand?

Keep your first attempt before consulting the [sample response and rubric](answers.md#activity-self-review). You can pause, save an open question and return later.
