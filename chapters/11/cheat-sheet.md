## Key distinctions

**Output, capability and asset.** A corrected result serves the present task; a capability belongs to the person who can apply the reasoning; an asset preserves useful work for reuse.

**Sustainable Leverage.** Build durable capability, useful assets and freedom without increasing physiological wear. A reusable file does not by itself establish demand for a business.

**Human Runtime and the human remainder.** Attention, energy and health are conditions of the workflow. Checking, adaptation, exceptions, support, maintenance and recovery still count.

**Net AI Value.** Use the heuristic to notice technical supervision, factual correction and behavioral regulation. It does not provide calibrated units for a universal score.

## Everyday example

A fictional teacher reuses a worksheet but spends longer repairing its assumptions than preparing a fresh one. Counting only drafting time hides the work needed to make the resource usable.

## Keep this question in view

Did I include checking, adaptation and support?
