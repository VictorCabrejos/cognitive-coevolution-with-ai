*When the artifact advances and the person does not*

## Key distinctions

**Gap, act and trajectory.** A beginner's gap is a present learning need. Judgment Abdication is a local surrender of required evaluation. AI-Assisted Epistemic Involution concerns increasing sophistication alongside declining understanding or control over time.

**Surface validation.** Appearance or a high score can distract from how the result was checked and whether its owner can change it.

**Post-hoc rationalization and Recursive dependency.** Generating a defense of a result can replace investigating a criticism; needing another generated defense for each follow-up can deepen dependency.

**Ownership checks.** Explain a choice, simplify it, change a condition and evaluate the result. Preserve uncertainty about claims not yet checked.

## Everyday example

A fictional household-budget sheet has many charts. Its owner can read the total but cannot explain one recurring cost. The gap calls for support. It does not, by itself, establish a history of declining capability.

## Keep this question in view

Can I explain each retained cost?
