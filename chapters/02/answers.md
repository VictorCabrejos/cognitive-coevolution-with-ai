### 4. Sample answer

Evidence over time of earlier understanding or control, increasingly sophisticated artifacts and weakening human ownership. Assistance, complexity or novice uncertainty alone does not establish that trajectory.

### 5. Sample answer

The proposed 10-person plan costs $40 + $30 + $25 + $20 = $115. The required 15-person plan costs $40 + $45 = $85. This does not prove the display has no value; it shows that its value needs justification against the brief. The attendance change and removal of extras are separate changes.

## Activity self-review

### One defensible response

Required cost for 10 attendees: $70. Proposed total: $115. Removing the two unrequired extras saves $45 for that attendance count. Required cost for 15 attendees: $85. A defensible explanation distinguishes the attendance change from the simplification and leaves the extras' possible benefits open.

### Five-point review

Give yourself one point for each criterion your work demonstrates. A different conclusion can earn the point when it meets the criterion and is supported by the supplied information.

1. Required and optional costs are separated.
2. The $70 and $115 calculations are correct.
3. The simplification is justified against the brief.
4. The changed required cost is $85 and is explained.
5. The explanation avoids diagnosing decline or declaring an untested benefit impossible.

**4–5:** identify one limit or improvement before reusing the work. **2–3:** revise one missing element and explain the change. **0–1:** return to the source card and work through one condition before expanding the task.

This is a practice aid, not a validated measure of competence, learning or health. Evaluate the reasoning rather than agreement with a philosophy or confidence in a response.
