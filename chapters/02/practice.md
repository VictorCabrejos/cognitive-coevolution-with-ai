*When the artifact advances and the person does not*

## Make one decision explainable

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Materials:** paper or a plain document; an assistant is optional.

### Source card

Fictional plan: a club spends $40 on a room and $3 per attendee on materials. A proposed budget for 10 people adds a $25 display and a $20 snack order. Neither extra is required by the brief: “Keep costs low while providing a room and materials.”

### Steps

1. Inspect (3 minutes): calculate the required room-and-materials cost. List the two additions separately.
2. Explain (3 minutes): give a reason for keeping or removing each addition. Mark any reason that needs information you do not have.
3. Simplify (3 minutes): make the smallest defensible plan that meets the brief. Compare its cost with the proposed plan.
4. Modify (4 minutes): without asking for a new finished budget, predict the required cost for 15 attendees. Check your prediction and explain what changed.
5. Teach back (2 minutes): explain the cost rule and one remaining uncertainty in ordinary language. An optional assistant may question your reasoning after your attempt; check its arithmetic yourself.

### Expected deliverable

A required-cost calculation, a justified simplification and an explanation of the changed case.

### Reflection checklist

- [ ] Can I explain each retained cost?
- [ ] Did I distinguish an unsupported addition from a proven mistake?
- [ ] Can I change the attendee count myself?
- [ ] Have I avoided inferring personal decline from one difficulty?

Keep your first attempt before consulting the [sample response and rubric](answers.md#activity-self-review). You can pause, save an open question and return later.
