# Chapter 9 practice

*Teach the loop, then test the learner*

[Chapter guide](README.md) · [Cheat sheet](cheat-sheet.md) · [Practice](practice.md) · [Self-check](self-check.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

## Keep the measure attached to the number

**Time:** about 18 minutes. **Materials:** paper or a plain document; an assistant is optional.

### Source card

Fictional Source A: 24 staff were invited to a workshop; 18 attended. Twelve attendees answered a survey, and nine said they felt more confident. No earlier confidence measure or independent skill test was collected. Before opening the review card, write the strongest claim these numbers support.

### Steps

1. First attempt (4 minutes): using only Source A, calculate the survey percentage and write a cautious conclusion. Keep this attempt before opening the review card below.
2. Inspect feedback (4 minutes): open the review card. Identify the population, outcome and adoption claim that the flawed summary changes or adds.
3. Repair (3 minutes): write a source-faithful claim and one useful next check. An optional assistant can criticize your repair; verify any suggestion against the memo.
4. Independent transfer (4 minutes): close the repair and use Source B from the review card. Write its strongest supported performance statement and two limits without asking for a finished answer.
5. Teaching note (3 minutes): identify whether your main difficulty was the denominator, the outcome measure or the scope of the conclusion. Propose a targeted next exercise and preserve why you chose it.

### Expected deliverable

A preserved first attempt, a corrected Source A claim, an independent Source B claim and a short teaching note.

### Reflection checklist

- [ ] Did I use respondents rather than all invitees as the denominator?
- [ ] Did confidence remain different from skill?
- [ ] Did I attempt the related case independently?
- [ ] Does the proposed next lesson address an observed difficulty?

Keep your first attempt before consulting the [sample response and rubric](answers.md#activity-self-review). You can pause, save an open question and return later.
