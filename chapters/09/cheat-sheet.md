## Key distinctions

**Activation Friction.** Remove obstacles such as inaccessible materials while leaving the target reasoning for the learner.

**Grounding / Source Anchoring.** Preserve the source's population, measure and conditions when making a claim.

**Cognitive Augmentation.** Inspect what the learner can explain or apply after assistance. Copying a corrected answer and reporting confidence answer different questions.

**Speed of Relevance.** Use a sufficiently supported observation to improve the next useful opportunity, then check the outcome. This does not justify rushing an unsupported claim.

## Everyday example

A fictional learner can compare a sentence with a memo but cannot find the file. Providing the memo removes an access obstacle. Deciding whether the sentence matches it still belongs in the learner's practice.
